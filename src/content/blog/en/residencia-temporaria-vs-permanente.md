One of the very first questions international newcomers ask is how temporary residency compares with permanent residency in Paraguay. Understanding the broad distinction makes planning much clearer.

## What temporary residency covers

Temporary residency is commonly the starting pathway for foreign nationals establishing lawful residence in Paraguay for the first time, supporting day-to-day living, remote work, or family life.

## What permanent residency covers

Permanent residency is aimed at long-term stability for residents committing to Paraguay over the years ahead, whether after completing initial residency stages or through specific eligible profiles.
